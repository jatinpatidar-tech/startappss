How our classes work

A teacher and a student use the Online

Whiteboard during a lesson.

The teacher teaches lessons to 2-3 students at a time.

Students don't see each other and

don't have any contact with each other.

The lesson is held individually,

not in a group: while one person is solving problems, we discuss the theory and methods

up to 90 min. lesson duration

This way, each student has time to review their homework, learn a new topic, and consolidate theory into practice.

80% of the lesson is covered in practice.

This approach is the most productive, as your child will learn to solve problems independently, and not just write down the solution as dictated by the teacher.
